Ledger — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Ledger ("the app") is a personal time-tracking and to-do app. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data.

Summary

Ledger does not collect, transmit, or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs of any kind. Everything you enter — time entries, categories, to-dos, and planned events — is stored locally in a database on your own device only.

Calendar access

If you choose to enable calendar sync, Ledger reads and writes events to a calendar you select on your device, using Apple's own Calendar system (EventKit). This lets your planned events show up in your device's calendar app, and through it, any account you've connected there yourself (for example Google Calendar). Ledger does not access your calendar unless you explicitly enable this feature and grant permission, and calendar data is exchanged directly between Ledger and your device's Calendar system — it is never sent to any server operated by the developer.

Data storage and deletion

All app data lives in a local database on your device. You can export a backup file at any time from within the app, and you can delete your data at any time by removing items in the app or by uninstalling the app, which permanently deletes the local database.

Third parties

Ledger does not share data with any third party, because it does not collect any data to share. The app contains no advertising or analytics libraries.

Children's privacy

Ledger does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, an updated version will be posted at this same URL with a new "Last updated" date.
