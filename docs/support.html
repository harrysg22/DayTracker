Ledger — Support

Have a question, found a bug, or want a feature?

Email waviapp.soporte@gmail.com — that's the fastest way to reach me. Please include your iOS version and, if it's a bug, what you were doing when it happened.

Do I need an account?

No. Ledger has no sign-in of any kind. Everything is stored locally on your device.

How do I back up or move my data?

Open the app's settings and use "Export Backup" to save a copy of your data as a file you can store or share. Backups can be restored from that same screen.

How does calendar sync work?

Ledger can sync your planned events with your device's calendar, which in turn can be connected to Google Calendar through iOS's own account settings. This is entirely optional and only happens if you enable it and grant calendar permission.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete individual items inside the app at any time. Deleting the app itself permanently removes all local data.
